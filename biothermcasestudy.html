Biotherm

Finding a growth opportunity in a consumer group often overlooked by skincare innovation.

The Opportunity

What if the opportunity wasn't another skincare expert, but someone who didn't know where to start?

The project investigated how Biotherm could grow within Australia's Gen X market while remaining aligned with its premium, science-led and sustainability positioning.

The Strongest Opportunity

Seeking Education + Support

Gen X consumers who hadn't heavily invested in skincare before, but were interested in beginning a more considered routine.

The Consumer Insight
Guidance

They are entering skincare rather than mastering it.

Trust

They are cautious purchasers who value product quality and reassurance before committing.

Convenience

They want skincare that is easy to understand, research and integrate into their routine.

The opportunity wasn't simply anti-ageing skincare.

It was making premium skincare easier to enter.

Strategic Direction
Acquire ↓ Educate ↓ Build the routine

The innovation should not operate as an isolated product. It should provide an accessible entry into Biotherm and create a pathway toward discovering the wider product ecosystem.

Final Concept

Microbiome Harmonising
Cleanser Tablets

A refillable tablet cleanser that dispenses one dose at a time and foams on contact with water, replacing the guesswork of a liquid cleanser with a single, pre-measured step.

Why This Direction
01 · Lower The Barrier To Entry

Target someone who needs support beginning skincare rather than competing only for highly involved skincare consumers.

02 · Build Around Trust

Use education and product experience to reduce uncertainty surrounding premium skincare.

03 · Design For Lifetime Value

Use the innovation as an entry into the broader Biotherm ecosystem rather than treating it as an isolated purchase.

04 · Stay Recognisably Biotherm

Ensure innovation remains consistent with Biotherm's premium, scientific and sustainability positioning.
