LEGO HOME

Concept campaign · Product innovation

“An exploration of what LEGO’s modular thinking could look like beyond the toy box: a furniture system designed to be assembled, reconfigured, moved and expanded.”

Self-initiated concept project. Not affiliated with, endorsed by, or produced by the LEGO Group.

The Challenge

Furniture is usually designed for a fixed room and a fixed purpose. For renters and young adults who move often, that can mean dismantling awkward flat-pack pieces, hauling bulky items between homes, or replacing furniture that no longer fits the next space.

What if furniture worked more like LEGO?

The Idea

The same modular kit reconfigures into different pieces as a space or need changes, without buying something new each time.

How It Works
Modular

Components can be rearranged into different configurations.

Portable

The system breaks down into individual panels for moving and storage.

Repairable

Individual components can be replaced instead of replacing the entire unit.

Expandable

Additional compatible modules can extend the system over time.

A proposed mechanism within the concept, not a production-engineered or technically validated design.

Audience & Positioning

Designed around renters and young adults living in smaller, temporary or frequently changing spaces. The idea is positioned around flexibility rather than cheap furniture. The product changes with the customer, instead of being replaced when their space or needs do.

The Model
Core kit ↓ Expansion modules ↓ Replacement components

The core kit is the entry point. Additional panels, functional modules and connectors give customers a reason to expand or reconfigure what they already own, rather than buy an entirely new piece of furniture.

The Campaign

MOVING?
TAKE IT APART.

The campaign turns the product's practical advantage into a line everyone who has moved house already understands. LEGO HOME simply asks people to imagine furniture that comes apart on purpose.
