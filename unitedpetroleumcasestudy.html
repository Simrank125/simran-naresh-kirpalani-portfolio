United Petroleum

Marketing Intern

United Petroleum was transitioning 200K+ fuel-discount customers from its existing card system to a new mobile app.

My work covered different parts of that transition, from reviewing the customer journey and planning migration communications to maintaining customer-facing station data and supporting the UP Community loyalty programme.

01

Customer Migration

United was moving existing fuel-discount customers off its card system and onto a new app, built with digital partner ID Digital. The discount itself didn't change, only how customers accessed it.

  • Reviewed the evolving app experience from the customer's side
  • Fed back on interface and customer journey changes
  • Contributed to journey development for new and existing users
  • Helped build the phased migration timeline
  • Weighed comms timing against technical delivery constraints
02

Communicating Through Change

The migration reached customers in four phases, each one messaging only the people who hadn't already moved across from the phase before.

500,000+ customer emails distributed across the migration
4 comms phases 20+ FAQs updated
Email ↓ Customer question ↓ FAQ / website
  • Helped write and edit the email copy for each phase
  • Worked on structure and tone across a large, mixed customer base
  • Updated and reorganised the FAQ content on the website
  • Made transition information easier for customers to find themselves
03

The Data Behind The Experience

Every fuel discount depends on the right station showing up in the right place, with the right hours. I cross-checked station information against Google and internal systems, and flagged discrepancies in details like location, operating hours and facilities, verifying with station managers and partners where needed.

500+ service stations checked
100+ discrepancies corrected

Also validated: 40+ club partner details as new clubs joined the program.
